WORK TO DISCUSS

What comes after a search in Korean?

  • A useful Korean-language business pageExplain owner-verified prices and visitor requirements in Korean. Review the questions people may use on Naver and other Korean-language search services.
  • A map visitors can use locallyConnect the Korean introduction to the actual local name and address. Confirm which map service works in the country and where the pin should be.
  • A booking path the business can supportCheck staff languages and time zones, existing booking tools, and the processes for confirmation, changes and cancellations.
Does a Korean page mean bookings are handled in Korean?

Not necessarily. The page language and the booking language may differ. Confirm Korean-language support with the person handling bookings; otherwise explain the contact methods visitors can actually use.

Map listings and booking integrations require a separate review of the local business’s eligibility, permissions and the service’s requirements.

Which countries do you support?

This proposal does not establish service availability in any country or the existence of an overseas office. Country-specific operating scope, contracting, payment arrangements, local language review and responsible staff need confirmation first.

The beauty or wellness business above is fictional. It does not represent a client, completed work, a recommendation by Korean visitors or a track record in any country.

Local contracting, payment and privacy requirements, and the countries we can serve, require review first. Creating Korean information does not mean taking over the local business’s booking or payment operations.
