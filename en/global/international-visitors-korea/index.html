WORK TO DISCUSS

Which pages need attention first?

  • Business and map informationCompare the business name, location and opening hours on the website and Google Maps. First confirm which listings the owner controls.
  • Details for each kind of visitRestaurants need menu and group-size information; cafés need seating and visitor policies; accommodation needs room and arrival details; beauty businesses need service inclusions and booking steps.
  • Booking instructions people can useState the languages and contact methods visitors can use, and explain how an inquiry becomes a confirmed booking.
Does an English menu mean English-speaking staff?

No. Confirm the language of the menu, the languages available for inquiries and the support available on site separately.

Connecting a new booking tool would require a separate review of the current booking process and the tool’s requirements.

Is the example a completed client project?

No. The restaurant example is fictional. It is not evidence of client work, delivery or results.

A proposed project may include verified operating information, agreed translation and review tasks, before-and-after page records and booking-path checks. Deliverables must be agreed first.

Markets, languages, translation review, photography, production and on-site visits are not yet committed. Actual scope and pricing require agreement before work begins.
