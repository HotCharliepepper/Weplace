START WITH THE FACTS

Before translating a page,
check what happens in person.

An English menu does not necessarily mean English-speaking staff. A Korean information page does not necessarily mean bookings can be made in Korean.

  • What the business actually offersVerify menus, prices, opening hours, location and visitor requirements with the owner.
  • How visitors can take the next stepSeparate available on-site languages, inquiry channels and booking confirmation methods.
  • What the project will includeAgree on the market, languages, pages, people, schedule and cost in a proposal.
How does this support search and AI discovery?

Align the business name, location and visitor information across the website and map listings. Build useful pages around questions customers ask, so people and search systems can understand the same facts.

We do not guarantee search rankings or inclusion in AI answers. Changes in searches and inquiries need agreed data sources and measurement rules.
