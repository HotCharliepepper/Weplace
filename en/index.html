Our interpretation
Keep essential facts and practical answers clear and consistent, including where people see them before visiting your website.

01. Consistent facts

Align business names, hours, location and services across your website, Naver Place and Google Business Profile.

02. Accessible answers

Publish prices, menus, booking conditions and FAQs as readable text in useful languages, alongside relevant photos.

03. Measurable change

Distinguish visibility, contact clicks, actual enquiries and bookings. A click alone is not a sale.
